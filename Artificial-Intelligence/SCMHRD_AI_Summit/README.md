## About the Project

SUSTAINiti is an AI-powered platform designed to help Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) automate business processes while simplifying ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) compliance, with a primary focus on the Social (S) pillar.

The project combines AI-driven workflow automation, process optimization, and intelligent reporting to reduce manual effort, improve operational efficiency, and generate BRSR-ready ESG insights. By making compliance faster, more affordable, and accessible, SUSTAINiti enables MSMEs to focus on sustainable growth rather than administrative complexity.

### Key Features

🤖 AI-powered business process automation
⚙️ Workflow optimization for MSMEs
📊 BRSR-ready ESG (Social) reporting
👥 Employee welfare and compliance insights
📈 Process analytics and operational efficiency
🌱 Sustainable business transformation

### Tech Stack

Artificial Intelligence
Workflow Automation
Business Process Optimization
ESG & BRSR Framework
Data Analytics

## Vision

Empowering MSMEs with intelligent automation and simplified ESG compliance to build efficient, resilient, and socially responsible businesses.
